## The Story
According to old legends from the Amt Rendsburg, a wealthy but notoriously greedy merchant once stood inside [[St. Marienkriche]] during a service, loudly mocking the pastor and the word of God. As divine retribution, a sudden, fatal stroke hit the blasphemer. He collapsed violently against a church pillar and died on the spot, his blood splattering across the stone.

While his body was removed, a deep red Blotplacken (bloodstain) remained on the wall. No matter how hard the sexton scrubbed it with water or tried to cover it with whitewash, the blood kept bleeding back through the lime after a few days. It served for centuries as a grim, supernatural warning to the townsfolk to respect the sanctuary. Though hidden by modern renovations today, the ghostly stain remains a staple of local folklore.
